TichuStats

The ultimate Tichu companion app.

TichuStats allows you to effortlessly track scores and analyze your Tichu gameplay. Gain insights into your performance with statistics across five different timeframes, completely free and ad-free.

Free and without Ads.

Score. Play. Edit.

Keep a detailed score of your Tichu games. players can enter scores from any device, and you can edit or delete rounds at any time, even after the game is finished.

Discover exciting Statistics.

Analyze your performance of Tichu games with 12+ statistics across five different timeframes and compare it with your friends.

Scroll Through Full History.

TichuStats keeps a detailed history of all your Tichu games. You can easily track who played bombs in each round or review how many Big Tichu you prevented in a single game.

Synced Across Devices.

TichuStats seamlessly synchronizes all your data. You can switch devices mid-game and continue right where you left off, with everything updating instantly.
